## The five open questions, settled

**(a) Does an untraded session burn one of the three carry sessions?** **YES — the clock counts CALENDAR sessions**, from the session the thesis first appears in a plan as a ranked trade or a named carry, whether or not a position was ever opened; that first appearance is session 1. The expressed-sessions-only reading would lengthen a thesis's life and is therefore a **LOOSEN**, which needs ≥ 10 journaled carries — today is sample 2 and loosening is not available. Consequences: **TEM is moot** (retired on the close, not on the clock); **IREN is at session 1 of 3**, so 2026-10-09 is session 2. Rule text changed in v8.

**(b) The deliberate 14:40 skip.** **Correct, and now written down.** 14:40 is a no-entry tick in the plan's own session rules; the entry window had closed at 13:12 on the TLT limb; CTVA was scrapped at 09:42; the book was flat; both seats would have had nothing to decide. It saved ~180–220k subagent tokens and Firecrawl credits the 15:40 and Coach ticks then needed, and the deviation was logged rather than hidden. **The runbook should say so explicitly** rather than leaving it to judgement — v8 now permits a directly-recorded tick under three named conditions. But the skip has a price, and today it was paid: it removed the only SPY read between 12:12 and 15:41, and the 770.44 low went unrecorded. v8 therefore binds a directly-recorded tick to refresh every live kill switch or mark it UNKNOWN.

**(c) The hardcoded budget at `Scripts/desk.workflow.js:461`.** Confirmed verbatim: `Budget: 1 scrape + 5 searches, one at a time, 'sleep 7' between.` It is why Seat 7 ignored the budget passed in `args` and spent 6 calls at 12:40 against a stated cap of 1. The channel that *does* reach every seat is `args.prior_notes` → the shared `CTX` at line 31, and it worked (Seat 7 spent 1 call at 13:40 and 0 at 15:40). **Read in credits the literal authorises 11 credits per tick, not 6 calls** — and line 452 (`7 scrapes`) is a second one. The one-line fix:

```js
// line 461
Budget: ${A.watch_budget || '1 scrape + 5 searches (= 11 Firecrawl credits: scrape 1, search 2 each)'}, one at a time, 'sleep 7' between. Alpha Vantage CAP: 0.
```

Read the literal from an optional arg with the current values as the default, so behaviour is byte-identical when the arg is absent; same pattern for line 452. **Yes, it should be applied before the next session** — it is the desk's largest uncontrolled credit draw on an account Firecrawl flagged as low on every response today. Not applied from here: it is a code edit to the live engine's execution path and the Coach writes no files. Handed to the orchestrator.

**(d) May a commodity switch block names with no exposure to that commodity?** For now, **yes — unchanged.** Scoping it is a **LOOSEN** of the desk's single highest-impact rule and the change policy requires ≥ 10 journaled samples; this is sample 1. And the sample is nearly worthless as evidence, because the switch **cost 0R**: CTVA's day high 14.47 never reached 14.62 and IREN's 38.06 never reached 38.08, so nothing it blocked was fillable. Being honest about the asymmetry the journal identified: once CTVA died at 09:42, no live switch could create a trade — every remaining switch could only block the one remaining short. What v8 *does* change is the bookkeeping: a book-wide switch must now record, per blocked name, whether that name had a live trigger and how far the last print was from it. That turns this from a recurring argument into a counted decision at 10 samples.

**(e) Is AUCTION_TAIL miscalibrated?** It is **mis-shaped, and one sample does not tell us it is mis-signed.** The switch needs TLT < 76.43 **AND** SPY < 773.61. Today SPY's limb *did* fire (low 770.44) while TLT rallied — day low 77.07, 0.64 above its 52W low, day high 77.73, 0.41 *through* the 77.32 risk-on level — through a +4–5% Brent move and a hawkish Fed backdrop (Waller, December-leaning minutes). So the AND never resolved, and the switch instead fired its own *inverse* no-new-shorts limb at 13:12. The defensible conclusion from one sample is **not** that oil-shock → higher-yields → TLT-down is wrong; it is that an AND across two instruments whose correlation is the very thing in question **cannot fire when they disagree — and disagreement is exactly when the regime is ambiguous and the desk most needs a verdict.** v8 therefore requires any two-instrument AND switch to name its disagreement case, defaulting to the conservative limb. The correlation assumption is left alone at sample 1.

# The seven seats

| # | Seat | Runs in | Reads | Writes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Screener | plan | finviz screens | watchlist |
| 2 | Macro / Regime | plan | web, finviz SPY | regime, levels, calendar |
| 3 | Technical | plan | stockanalysis/finviz quote pages, AV 5-min bars | levels, setups, grades |
| 4 | Catalyst / News | plan | web, AV earnings calendar | catalysts, earnings |
| 5 | Head Trader | plan, premarket, monitor | all seats + [[Rules]] | playbook, re-levels, paper orders |
| 6 | Risk Manager | plan | playbook | verdicts (binding) |
| 7 | Smart Money & Power | plan, monitor | finviz insider feed, AV congress/insider, web (White House, Musk, DOJ/SEC/OFAC, Gulf) | flags, alerts |
| — | Coach | coach (nightly) | plan, journal, actual 5-min bars, [[Rules]] | [[Reviews]], [[Lessons]], rule changes |

Modes: `plan` (evening), `premarket` (09:00 ET), `monitor` (every 30 min 09:30–16:00 ET), `coach` (16:30 ET).

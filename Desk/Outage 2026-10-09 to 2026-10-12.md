## The expired runs were NOT executed, deliberately

Fifteen of these are Friday's intraday ticks. Running them now would write a session that the desk did not trade, from quotes taken three days later, into Friday's journal. That is the exact defect Rules v6 was written against ("every record carries its session date"), and it would put fabricated rows in the ledger the Coach replays. They are recorded here as lost and left unrun. Friday's scorecard row says *no session*, not zero R.
